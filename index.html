What changed (quick)

Short summary & test checklist for the 3D upgrade (keeps all original text/content).

  • Re-skinned the UI into the red/black/white palette you requested.
  • Built an embedded WebGL scene (textured sphere avatar, starfield, animated red rim/bloom) — no external libs.
  • Interactive camera: mouse/touch dragging, scroll zoom, keyboard arrows + R to reset.
  • Toggle to quickly switch 3D on/off and a reduce-motion toggle for accessibility.

Quick test checklist

  1. Open the page — the 3D scene should render behind content with a starfield and glowing red rim.
  2. Drag the mouse (or touch-drag) near the avatar to rotate; scroll to zoom in/out.
  3. Press arrow keys to rotate slightly, press 'R' to reset the camera, press Enter to toggle 3D on/off.
  4. Click "Reduce Motion" to reduce animations and glow for accessibility.
