Case Study: Advanced Multi-threaded Port Scanner

Context & Problem

During security auditing, standard scanning tools can be noisy or slow. A lightweight, customised Python-based scanner was required to quickly identify open TCP ports and grab banners across a network without the overhead of heavy frameworks.

Architecture & Flow

The tool utilises Python's native socket library to establish connections and threading to perform concurrent scanning. It maps ports, attempts basic banner grabbing for service identification, and outputs results cleanly to the terminal.

Results

Successfully reduced scan time on localised networks by implementing thread pooling, allowing for faster reconnaissance phases during lab assessments.

Case Study: ESP8266 Wi-Fi Deauthentication Attack

Context & Problem

Understanding wireless vulnerabilities is crucial for securing enterprise networks. This project demonstrates how inexpensive IoT hardware can disrupt 802.11 communications if networks are not configured with management frame protection (802.11w).

Methodology

Flashed an ESP8266 micro-controller with Deauther firmware. Operated within a strict, controlled lab environment to send spoofed deauthentication packets (management frames) from the access point to targeted client devices, forcing disconnection.

Lessons Learned

Demonstrated the critical flaw in older Wi-Fi standards where management frames are sent unencrypted. Validated the necessity of WPA3 and 802.11w for modern network deployments to mitigate localised denial-of-service attacks.
